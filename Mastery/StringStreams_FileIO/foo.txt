Set during the final days of the Clone Wars, the film opens with Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi rescuing Chancellor Palpatine, while Anakin is increasingly tormented by visions of his secret wife Padmé dying in childbirth. 
Palpatine, secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, exploits Anakin’s fear and his growing distrust of the Jedi Council by promising that the dark side holds the power to prevent death. 
When Mace Windu tries to arrest Palpatine, Anakin intervenes to save him, pledges himself to the Sith as Darth Vader, and leads the slaughter of the Jedi Temple as clone troopers carry out Order 66 across the galaxy. 
Palpatine declares himself Emperor, turning the Republic into the Galactic Empire, while Obi-Wan defeats Anakin in a brutal duel on Mustafar and leaves him burned, after which Anakin is rebuilt in the iconic black armor. 
Padmé dies after giving birth to twins Luke and Leia, who are hidden apart on Tatooine and Alderaan, setting the stage for the original trilogy.
